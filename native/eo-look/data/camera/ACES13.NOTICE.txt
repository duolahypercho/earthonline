Changes from the source: translated from CTL to Rust; forward direction
only; the RRT and that one ODT are fused; the matrices are computed from the
primaries (aces2.rs) rather than copied. Nothing of the CTL is redistributed
except as this translation; the CTL files were read as text and are not
stored in this repository.

The virtual test chart the camera look's tests use (camera.rs
`NEUTRAL_RAMP`, `chart_patches`) is analytic (a grey ramp and CIELAB
patches at fixed lightness and chroma) and carries no third-party data; the
BabelColor ColorChecker values earlier tests used were removed.
