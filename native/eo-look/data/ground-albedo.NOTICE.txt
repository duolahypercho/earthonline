ground-albedo - the per-class ground albedos in native/eo-look/src/phys.rs

No data file is shipped: the values are constants in phys.rs (`SEA_ALBEDO`, `TREE_ALBEDO`,
`LOW_VEGETATION_ALBEDO`, `BARE_ALBEDO`), applied to the classes of the shipped land cover
(public/data/sf/sf-land-cover.bin.gz, ESA WorldCover 2021 v200, CC BY 4.0; see its own NOTICE).
This file records where each value comes from and how far that source was checked.

Sea, 0.06 (the figures as the earlier pass took them from these papers; the citations are
confirmed, the full texts were not re-read for this file):
  Payne, R. E., 1972: Albedo of the sea surface. J. Atmos. Sci. 29, 959-970
    (monthly mean ocean albedo about 0.06 at solar altitudes over about 30 degrees).
  Jin, Z., T. P. Charlock, W. L. Smith Jr. and K. Rutledge, 2004: A parameterization of ocean
    surface albedo. Geophys. Res. Lett. 31, L22301 (about 0.066 under a diffuse sky).

Tree cover 0.05 and grass, shrub, crop and wetland 0.06 (INFERRED, source not read):
  Meant as the MODIS MCD43 visible-band (0.3-0.7 um) white-sky means of the forest and grassland
  IGBP classes. The per-class table these should come from (Gao, F., C. B. Schaaf et al., 2005:
  MODIS BRDF and albedo Climate Modeling Grid products and the variability of albedo for major
  global vegetation types. J. Geophys. Res. 110, D01104) could not be read: the publisher's page
  refused access and no open full text was found. The values are therefore INFERRED and owed a
  primary source. An earlier comment cited a bare-soil albedo paper for the forest value and a
  second-hand quote for the grass value; both citations were withdrawn.

Bare ground: the city's mean (`URBAN_GROUND_ALBEDO`), INFERRED.

Deviation from the plan: the plan asks for a broadband albedo; the values are visible-band,
because the renderer's light is visible light and a canopy's near-infrared reflectance does not
light a wall. San Francisco's summer-dry grass is lighter than the green-grass value; no seasonal
value is applied.
