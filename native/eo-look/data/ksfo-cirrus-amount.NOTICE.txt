ksfo-cirrus-amount.csv - how much of the sky San Francisco airport's cirrus days cover, by month

Source: routine hourly METAR reports of San Francisco International Airport (KSFO), 2014-2023,
  from the NOAA / National Weather Service Automated Surface Observing System (ASOS),
  as archived by the Iowa Environmental Mesonet (Iowa State University),
  https://mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/request/download.phtml
Licence: the observations are works of the US federal government (NOAA/NWS) and are in the
  public domain in the United States. The archive is credited as the Iowa Environmental Mesonet.
Query:         the same file as ksfo-clear-day-sky.csv (see ksfo-clear-day-sky.py)
Source sha256: 108ab81bc11ac6cf8ef58b97fee005e138fea602cef8709dd3c7ccab1bf50e54 (87,616 lines, 87,615 reports)
Produced by:   native/eo-look/data/ksfo-clear-day-sky.py --amount, which refuses an input with another digest.
               The input file is not tracked; the script finds it through EO_KSFO_METAR_CSV.

Kept: per month, the cirrus days of ksfo-clear-day-sky.csv counted by the day's high-cloud
amount: the METAR sky-cover code (FEW, SCT, BKN, OVC) of the most covering layer at or over
18,000 ft, the median over the day's reports that carry one. The codes are eighths of the sky
(FEW 1-2 oktas, SCT 3-4, BKN 5-7, OVC 8: WMO-No. 306 FM 15 METAR regulation 15.9.1.1).
Read by: native/eo-sky/src/clouds.rs (`ksfo_cirrus_amounts`, `drawn_oktas`, `modal_oktas`).
The cirrus heights in these reports are observer estimates (the ASOS ceilometer does not reach
cirrus); they are not used.
