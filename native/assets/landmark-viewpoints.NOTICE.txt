landmark-viewpoints.json

What: named places a visitor stands at San Francisco landmarks (fast
travel arrivals, bot destinations), as WGS84 latitude and longitude with
the bearing a visitor faces and a spread radius.

Source: every coordinate is derived from OpenStreetMap data already in this
repository (public/data/sf/sf-city.json.gz: the named ways and building
footprints each entry's "source" field names, by OSM id), converted with
that file's projection (meta.center 37.778 N, 122.4194 W; 110574 m per
degree of latitude, 87986.25 m per degree of longitude). The Crissy Field
entry's bearing faces the Golden Gate Bridge's south tower, 37.8107 N,
122.4776 W.

https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. The derivation is described in
each entry; no imagery was used.
