# Sound credits

Earth Online's recorded sounds come from two collections. Generated by `native/tools/write-audio-manifest.mjs`; do not edit by hand.

- **FSD50K** (Eduardo Fonseca, Xavier Favory, Jordi Pons, Frederic Font, Xavier Serra, "FSD50K: an Open Dataset of Human-Labeled Sound Events", 2020; https://zenodo.org/records/4060432), released under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Each clip is a Freesound recording under its own licence; only CC0 and CC BY clips are used. Audio was taken from the mirror `Fhrozen/FSD50k@ccf1acaff12f3f4a4c10052dddafbb6d22152c9b` and checked against the upstream clip IDs and the mirror's SHA-256.
- **SFX Kit by Wyzowl** (https://github.com/wyzowl/sfx-kit), CC0 1.0: the interface sounds.

Changes: the game plays these clips trimmed (single footsteps cut from walking recordings, loops taken from engine recordings), mixed to mono, level-matched, pitched and filtered in its 3D mixer. CC0 clips need no credit; their uploaders are listed in `MANIFEST.tsv` beside the audio.
