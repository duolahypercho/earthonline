Produced by the project's earlier data pipeline (generator recorded in meta); that
script is not part of this repository.

Contents: 276 footprints in the sf-city frame (x east, z south, metres), each with
its block/lot key, street address (275 of 276), height from the footprint dataset's
LiDAR fields, and assessor-roll civic attributes (storeys, year built, use code,
units, construction and property class). Ids are 8e9 + block/lot.
DataSF footprints are shifted per area onto the runtime footprints (median of
one-to-one neighbour pairs, recorded in meta.offsets) and trimmed off every runtime
footprint they would overlap; no OpenStreetMap geometry is copied into this file.
