This file is a Derivative Database of OpenStreetMap: amenity=parking areas,
leisure/landuse/natural green areas and inland standing water (lakes, ponds,
open reservoirs, lagoons), re-projected to world metres. No names are
carried. Any public use must keep this attribution and share derivative
databases under the ODbL.

Inland water is kept only where its middle stands on the land of sf-shoreline.geojson
(DataSF, PDDL 1.0; sha256 a3023288edff7a91f84f20ca54fc55693b2f6a4fa4fb396807378f31be80f01d), so the bay's channels stay the sea's. Nothing of that file is copied.
