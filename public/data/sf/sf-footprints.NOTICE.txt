Contents: the outer ring of each footprint (largest polygon), re-projected to
the extract's world metres (sf-frame-south-v1), rounded to 0.1 m, repeated and
collinear vertices removed (Douglas-Peucker 0.25 m), simple rings only; height
from the dataset's LiDAR fields (hgt_median_m; 0.85 x the first-return peak for
towers whose median undercounts, unless a road deck stands over the footprint,
whose first returns are the deck). Footprints whose centroid an OpenStreetMap
footprint of sf-city.json already covers are left out, so the two never draw one
building twice. No names or addresses are carried.
